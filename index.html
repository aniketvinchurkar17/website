IThings I built

Each one exists because I wanted an answer and couldn’t find it.

  1. Real Ratelive ↗

    A salary converter that argues back. Nine questions about how you actually spend become your own consumption basket; it then tells you what you would need to earn there to live the way you live here, after tax on both sides.

  2. IC Memo Auto-drafteragents

    Type a company name, get an investment committee memo in about two minutes. One agent researches in a loop, one drafts, one tears the draft apart as a sceptical partner.

  3. UnDochrome extension

    A to-do list that will not let you write a task down until you have said whether it is urgent and whether it is important.
